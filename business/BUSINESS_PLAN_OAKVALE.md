## 1. Business Overview

### What We Do
Ubuntu Health Vault is a blockchain-powered medical records platform that gives patients control over their health data while enabling clinics and doctors to securely access verified medical histories.

Patients can approve or deny access to their records using:
- Smartphone app
- SMS
- USSD

### The Problem
South Africa's healthcare system is fragmented. Clinics and hospitals often cannot share patient records, especially in the public sector.

This leads to:
- Lost or incomplete medical histories
- Repeated tests and delays
- Misdiagnosis and poor treatment decisions
- Paper records being damaged or lost

Patients also have limited control over who accesses their personal medical information.

### Our Solution
Ubuntu Health Vault provides a secure, patient-controlled health record system.

Key features include:
- Patients own and control access to records
- Doctors request access digitally
- Patients approve access via app, SMS, or USSD
- Every access request is logged permanently
- Access can expire automatically or be revoked at any time
- Records are encrypted before storage

Platform components:
- Patient portal
- Doctor portal
- Clinic admin dashboard

### Technology and Automation
The platform uses automation to improve healthcare workflows:
- Smart contracts automatically manage permissions and access expiry
- SMS and USSD notifications support users without internet access
- AES-256 encryption protects uploaded records
- Blockchain audit logs track all access activity

### AI Features
The platform includes AI-powered support tools:
- Pre-consultation summaries for doctors
- Automated follow-up and referral scheduling
- Preventive care reminders and check-up recommendations

## 2. Market Opportunity

### Target Customers
Initial focus:
- Community clinics
- NGO healthcare facilities
- Public primary care clinics

These facilities:
- Serve large patient volumes
- Often lack modern record systems
- Need affordable digital solutions

Secondary market:
- Occupational health clinics in mining and manufacturing

### Market Size
- Approximately 3,900 clinics and 406 hospitals in South Africa
- Approximately 48 million public healthcare users
- South African digital health market projected to reach **USD 1.4 billion by 2030**

### Why Now
Three major trends support market timing:
- POPIA compliance pressure is increasing demand for secure patient data systems
- Low-cost blockchain infrastructure now makes secure audit logging affordable
- High mobile phone penetration enables nationwide SMS and USSD access
